AI-Assisted Drafting
for CCH iFirm

Removing the blank page from client documents

3/3Customers validated the concept
ShippedCanadian market
1CCH iFirm
Systems thinking Product strategy Human AI system MVP definition Stakeholder alignment
Built inside CCH iFirm at Wolters Kluwer, their cloud practice management product for accounting and tax firms, and released in the Canadian market. Some of this work cannot be shown as it was built. The interfaces here are redrawn and the data is altered; the decisions, constraints and outcomes are not.
01
The context

Our users were already using AI. They were just using it outside the product.

The brief was about editing. The real problem was starting.

The research insight

Professionals already used ChatGPT to draft emails and written content during their working day.

Product problem

Our product still gave them a blank page to start from.

Design opportunity

Bring that behaviour into the product. Improving the editor would make the blank page slightly better. AI-assisted drafting could remove it.

Research method
  • 5Foundational research sessions conducted in 2024 on AI and usage among Wolters Kluwer customers

The instruction panel

Intent in plain language. No prompt fields and no model picker: choosing the model is the product's job, not the professional's. The panel states what it will not do before it does anything.

Accessibility, written into the spec

Three of the notes the specification carries, so they are built rather than retrofitted.

  • 01The panel is a labelled region, not a dialog. It does not trap focus, and the document stays readable and editable behind it.
  • 02Generation progress is announced politely, so a screen reader hears that work is happening without the draft interrupting whatever is being read.
  • 03Colour is never the only signal. The rail that marks machine-written text is paired with a chip that says so in words.
07
MVP definition

Not everything useful belongs in the first release

ShippedInstruction-based AI drafting
ShippedFull human review before saving or sending
DeferredReusable templates for generated documents
Why templates were deferred

Templates introduced a second lifecycle: ownership, versioning, editing rights and what happens when the underlying rules change.

Decision

Validate the core drafting behaviour first. A smaller MVP gave us a safer way to validate the core behaviour before introducing lifecycle complexity.

How I would measure it

Are people reading what the AI wrote?

No product analytics were available to me on this work, so this is the plan rather than the result. Written as it would go to the team.

Target behaviourStarting a document from a draft instead of an empty page, and editing it before approving
Event to instrumentDrafts generated, drafts edited before save, drafts discarded without saving
The questionWhat proportion of generated drafts are edited before approval?
At thirty daysA high edit rate is the good outcome here: it means people are reading. A high discard rate points at the instruction step, not the model

Business and delivery

A validated MVP released in Canada

3 of 3 customers validated the concept. Users needed to accelerate their workflow with AI.

After release

I thought this was a tool for contracts. People used it for anything that needed a signature.

My working assumption throughout design was that the feature served formal, structured documents: engagement letters, contracts, the heavyweight paperwork of a practice.

In use, the pattern was broader. People reached for it for anything that required a client signature, including short, routine correspondence. The opportunity was wider than the brief, and wider than my own framing of it.
